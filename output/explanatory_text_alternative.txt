This line chart shows the percentage of patients meeting the weekly 150-minute exercise goal across four follow-up visits for the Home-based and Center-based programs. The x-axis shows follow-up visits 1 through 4, and the y-axis shows the percentage of patients seen at each visit who met the goal. Home-based increases from 58% at visit 1 to 70% at visit 4, while Center-based increases from 57% to 79%. At visit 4, Center-based is 9 percentage points higher than Home-based, 79% versus 70%. Patients chose their program, so this comparison does not show that either program causes more exercise. Each percentage describes only the patients seen at that visit, about 45 per program at visit 4, so the 9-point gap could also reflect chance or differences in who returned for follow-up.
